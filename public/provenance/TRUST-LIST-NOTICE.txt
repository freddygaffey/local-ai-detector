C2PA Trust List (bundled for offline Content Credentials validation)
=====================================================================

Files:
  C2PA-TRUST-LIST.pem      Trust anchors for C2PA claim-signing certificates
  C2PA-TSA-TRUST-LIST.pem  Trust anchors for C2PA time-stamping authorities
                           (bundled for reference/future use; not currently
                           passed to the validator, see src/provenance/c2pa.ts)

Source:   https://github.com/c2pa-org/conformance-public/tree/main/trust-list

The files are redistributed unmodified. They are used only locally, inside
the extension, to decide whether a Content Credentials signer chains to a
C2PA-conformant certificate authority. Refresh them with each release.

The frozen C2PA Interim Trust List (contentcredentials.org, pre-2026 signers)
is NOT bundled: its redistribution licence could not be confirmed. Assets
signed only under the interim list therefore show as "valid signature,
signer not on the C2PA Trust List".
